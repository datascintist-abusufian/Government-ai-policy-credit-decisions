KEY DIRECT FINDINGS FROM THE PROVIDED STATA CODE

1. POST-ORIGINATION DATE DERIVATIONS. Lines 11-23 of P2P_Macro_Codes.do:
   issue_date is parsed from issue_d; last_date is parsed from last_pymnt_d; missing last_date is filled with issue_date.
   quarter, month and year are then calculated from daily_date = dofm(last_date).
   Thus the provided generation logic for model predictors year, month, quarter depends on last payment timing, not the application date.
   issue_year, separately, is based on issue_d, described in Labels.do line 36 as the month the loan was FUNDED.

2. POST-ORIGINATION LOAN-VOLUME DERIVATIONS. Lines 74-81 of P2P_Macro_Codes.do:
   loan_vol is built by grouping state and last_date and aggregating loan_amnt. Because last_date comes from last_pymnt_d, this is post-origination information.
   median_dummy is built from loan_vol. If the dataset feature logloan_vol is derived from loan_vol, it inherits the concern, but the provided .do file does not show its definition.

3. NON-TRAILING SAME-MONTH AGGREGATE. Line 75 of P2P_Macro_Codes.do:
   loan_vol6m is grouped by state and issue_date and sums loan_amnt for the group. Despite its name, this code is NOT a trailing six-month calculation.
   loglvol_6m is log(loan_vol6m) (line 76). Same-month aggregates can depend on loan originations later in that month.

4. ROW-SHIFTED FIELDS (UNVERIFIED TIME LAGS). Lines 69-73 of P2P_Macro_Codes.do:
   muni_6m and inf_6m are replaced by observations six ROWS earlier, not explicitly six calendar months earlier. Correct time alignment cannot be assumed from this code.

5. FUNDING/STAGE-DEPENDENT FIELDS. Lines 41-47 of P2P_Macro_Codes.do:
   logfunded_amnt is explicitly the log of funded_amnt. The latter is the committed loan amount (Labels.do line 24).
   funded_amnt_inv describes investor commitments (Labels.do line 25); grade/sub_grade, interest rate and installment can be set during underwriting/funding.
   Their permissible use depends on whether the intended prediction is at application, pricing or post-funding monitoring.

6. OUTCOME TARGET. Lines 32-38 of P2P_Macro_Codes.do:
   badloan=1 for Charged Off, Default, policy-related Charged Off, and two Late statuses. All other statuses are assigned badloan=0, including Current.
   This is a mixed recorded-status endpoint, not a common fixed-horizon default measure. Its snapshot timestamp is not established by these files.

7. HISTORICAL MACRO VARIABLES AND FAIRNESS PROXIES:
   This code does not establish release/publication timing for GDP, CPI, unemployment or other aggregated covariates.
   Geographic/political/religiosity variables may introduce proxy and fairness risks independently of leakage; this cannot be resolved by their names alone.
