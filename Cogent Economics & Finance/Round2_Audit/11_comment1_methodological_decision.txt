D. METHODOLOGICAL DECISION
==================================================

1. The 2019 mature test cohort is selected
   entirely through recorded terminal status.

2. The mature training cohort also includes
   nonterminal observations selected through
   contractual age.

3. Contractual age does not demonstrate that
   the recorded outcome has been followed
   through the contractual period.

4. The dataset does not currently establish
   uniform historical observation dates.

5. Replacing the test cohort without verifying
   outcome availability would not solve the
   fundamental evaluation problem.

6. Reliable historical outcome snapshots or
   event dates would be needed for a stronger
   full-cohort risk evaluation.

7. Until then, original prediction results
   must be interpreted conditionally.

==================================================
E. PUBLICATION CAUTION
==================================================

This report is an audit finding.

It is not evidence that every Current loan
subsequently defaulted.

It does not quantify actual label errors.

It does not prove that inconsistent labels
caused the model's calibration errors.

The existing results should not be presented
as representative full-cohort default-risk
performance without additional validation.
